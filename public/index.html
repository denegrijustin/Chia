How to care

Plant seeds and give your pet a drink. Quick mode grows a full coat in about two minutes with sunlight and moisture. Slow mode takes about two hours.

  • Water boosts moisture. Give each drink 15 seconds to soak in.
  • Plant food adds growth for 5 leaves.
  • Keep moisture above 15%. Shade slows growth.
  • Trim a fluffy pet to earn 15 leaves and start again.
  • Your garden grows while you’re away, up to 8 hours. Progress stays in this browser.
